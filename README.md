MediShield AI - Digital Contact Tracing
MDR-Shield is a smart digital hospital infection-control system designed to detect, trace, and contain the spread of Multi-Drug Resistant (MDR) pathogens in real time.
The platform integrates with hospital records, lab reports, and patient-staff interaction data to automatically trace contact chains, assess exposure risk, and generate early alerts for infection-control teams.
By replacing slow and error-prone manual tracking with an AI-driven, real-time monitoring dashboard, MDR-Shield helps hospitals prevent outbreaks early, improve patient safety, and reduce healthcare costs while supporting faster and more effective containment decisions.
